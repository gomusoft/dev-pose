# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and productive environment for everyone — regardless of experience level, background, or role.

---

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind, constructive, and professional in all communications
- Give and receive feedback with openness and humility
- Acknowledge mistakes, learn from them, and move on
- Prioritize the team's goals over personal preferences
- Help onboard newer members and share knowledge freely

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Dismissive, demeaning, or disrespectful language (in code, reviews, or chat)
- Personal attacks or harassment of any kind
- Sharing sensitive information (credentials, PII) carelessly
- Submitting work you know is broken without flagging it

---

## Code Review Standards

Good code reviews are as important as good code.

**As a reviewer:**
- Comment on the code, not the person
- Explain *why* something should change, not just *what*
- Approve things that are good enough — perfect is the enemy of done
- Respond to PR reviews within **1 business day**

**As an author:**
- Don't take feedback personally
- Respond to all comments, even if just "acknowledged" or "done"
- Keep PRs small and focused (< 400 lines where possible)

---

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this code of conduct, report it to a team lead or repository admin privately.

All reports will be handled confidentially and promptly.
